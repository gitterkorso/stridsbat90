# Reference: cutaway illustration of Stridsbåt 90 H "845" (illustrator: Borg)

The initial user reference (2026-09-06) was this illustration. That original scope has since been refined by the
2026-10-20 Swedish-H/925 source hierarchy above. The historic observations below were read off the illustration
(viewed from the port quarter, port side cut away, bow to the right) and converted
into the project frame: metres, Y up, +X bow, +Z starboard, origin transom × waterline × centreline, hull x 0..14.9.
Positions are estimates (±0.3 m); shapes and relative placement are what matters.

## Hull and paint
* Hard-chine planing hull, truncated bow with the stem door (stävport), black antifouling below the boot-top, splinter
  camouflage in olive / grey-green / dark / light tones. Rubbing strake all round; bow fender pad.
* Hull number **845** on both bows and on the transom. Correction 2026-10-20: Borg shows **dark digits on pale camouflage fields**, not the light-grey digits previously claimed here. Number placement remains reconstructed.

## Stern (x < 0)
* Two waterjets with steering nozzles and reversing buckets, exhausts on the transom.
* A tubular **stern guard frame** ("akterbåge"): horizontal tube (r ≈ 0.03) at about y 0.5 wrapping from the transom
  corner aft around the jet and back, with diagonal braces to the transom and a short vertical at the aft corner; a
  **boarding ladder** (two rails + rungs) hanging from the frame down toward the water on the port side.

## Aft deck (akterdäck, x 0..5.2, y 1.15)
* Railing all round: stanchions (~0.9 m) with a top rail and one intermediate rail along both sides AND across the
  transom.
* **Flag**: a large Swedish flag (≈1.0 × 0.62 m, blue 0x0e5cad with yellow cross, proportions 16:10, cross bars 2/10 of
  the height) on a ≈1.6 m staff at the **port aft corner** (x ≈ 0.35, z ≈ −1.35), flying aft.
* **Louvred ventilation box** (engine air intake) on the port side aft of the step: grey box ≈1.3 × 0.55 × 0.45 m with
  horizontal louvres on its outboard face (x ≈ 3.6..4.9, z ≈ −1.2).
* Two **grey stowage boxes** with rounded lids (≈0.9 × 0.5 × 0.55 m) on the starboard side (x ≈ 0.4..1.3 and 3.75..4.65,
  z ≈ +1.2). No white life-raft cylinders in the picture.
* Two engine-room working hatches in the deck (flush lids) – keep.
* A **lifebuoy** (orange ring ≈0.65 m outer diameter with white bands) hung on the port railing (x ≈ 3.0).
* A tall **whip antenna** (≈3 m) near the port side of the step (x ≈ 5.0, z ≈ −1.5).
* At the step up to the mid-deck: the troop-compartment hatch lid standing open (hinged forward), and a **doorway with a
  ladder** in the step face down into the troop compartment (port of centre).

## Engine room (x 0.6..4.0)
* Two **orange Scania V8** diesels (0xd5601f) side by side (z ≈ ±0.62, x ≈ 1.3..3.0): block, two angled cylinder banks
  with valve covers, turbo on top, exhaust manifolds, flywheel housing aft, shafts to the jets. Visible in the cutaway /
  through the open hatches.

## Mid-deck (mellandäck, x 5.2..8.6, y 1.95)
* Railing along both sides of the mid-deck (stanchions ≈0.9 m, top + intermediate rail) from the step to the wheelhouse.
* **Ring mount (ringlavett)** centred x ≈ 7.3: ring r ≈ 0.55 at ≈1.0 m above the deck (y ≈ 2.95) carried by **four tubes
  converging down to a central pedestal / base plate** (pyramid), gunner's step/seat bar, ammunition box on the base;
  12.7 mm M2 on a pintle post on the ring's forward side, **pointing forward and elevated ≈20°** (over the wheelhouse
  roof).
* Second **lifebuoy** on the starboard mid-deck railing (x ≈ 6.6).

## Wheelhouse (styrhytt, x 8.6..11.75)
* Historic height estimate y 3.0 was superseded in October; consult current constants and audit. **Front glazing leans FORWARD** (top edge forward of the sill). Correction 2026-10-20: the governing 925 photo supports **three separate front panes with a clear central pane**; the former “three centre plus two corner” count was not reliable. The lower front below the sill is a
  camo-painted plate sloping DOWN and FORWARD to the foredeck (the cabin front is a "Z" in profile: foot on the foredeck
  at x ≈ 11.75, sill at x ≈ 11.25 y 2.40, glass top at x ≈ 11.55 y 3.0). Side walls: two large side panes per side whose
  forward edge follows the forward lean; small aft-facing windows. Slight tumblehome is fine.
* No raised deck / gun well in front of the windscreen: the foredeck runs straight to the cabin foot.
* Roof: tubular handrail around the roof edge; **mast** (≈2.2 m pole with masthead light, small camera/sensor box and
  antennas) at the **aft port corner**; **radar** = a compact flat-array antenna in a rectangular housing (≈0.75 × 0.22 ×
  0.12 m) on a short pedestal at the **aft starboard corner** of the roof, rotating; two small **searchlights** at the roof
  front; horn; GPS dome. No radar arch.
* Interior: helm (wheel) starboard, two pilot seats + one centre seat behind, console with screens under the windscreen.

## Foredeck (fördäck, x 11.75..14.9, at sheer level, sloping gently to the stem)
* **Current foredeck correction, 2026-10-20:** 925 shows short grab handles beside the bow hatches and a nearly horizontal central hoop projecting from the roof, supported by converging deck braces. The old claim of continuous port/starboard foredeck rails is not the governing arrangement.
* Two-part **bow hatch** on the centreline (each leaf hinged outboard) over the landing corridor; stem door/ramp.
* **Twin 12.7 mm MG** on a **pedestal** on the **starboard** foredeck just forward of the wheelhouse (x ≈ 12.3, z ≈ +0.85),
  barrels forward above the rail; elevation articulated (−5..+30°).

## Troop compartment (x 4.0..11.4, floor −0.30)
* **Corrected 2026-10-20:** SHK RM 2008:02, printed p.12, describes **nine inward-facing seats along each side plus two forward-facing seats against the aft bulkhead**, totalling 20 for the older Swedish H arrangement. The previous four-across/five-row interpretation was false and must not be used. SHK 2026:02 documents a later 9+9 configuration; it does not justify rotating the older seats forward. Cushion shapes, mounts and precise spacing remain reconstructions. Bulkhead with WT door forward, engine-room bulkhead aft.
